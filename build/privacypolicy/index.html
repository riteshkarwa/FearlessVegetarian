Privacy Policy

At Fearless Vegetarian, we respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit or use the Fearless Vegetarian website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the website or the Scratch Pad. Please do not enter sensitive or confidential information into the Scratch Pad.

Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation

The words whose initial letters are capitalized have meanings defined below. These definitions apply whether the words appear in singular or plural form.

Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

  • Company means Fearless Vegetarian, also referred to as "We," "Us," or "Our."

  • Cookies are small files that may be placed on your computer, mobile device, or other device by a website.

  • Personal Data means information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.

  • Service refers to the Fearless Vegetarian website.

  • Service Provider means a third party that processes information on behalf of Fearless Vegetarian to help provide, maintain, analyze, or improve the Service.

  • Usage Data refers to information that may be collected automatically when you use the Service, such as technical and website usage information.

  • Website refers to Fearless Vegetarian, accessible from https://fearlessvegetarian.netlify.app/

  • You means the individual accessing or using the Website.

Information We May Collect

Personal Information

Fearless Vegetarian does not require visitors to provide their real name, email address, telephone number, or mailing address simply to browse the website.

Some optional features may allow visitors to voluntarily provide limited information. For example, the Scratch Pad feature allows a visitor to choose a username so that saved Scratch Pad content can be associated with that username.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the website or the Scratch Pad.

Scratch Pad

Fearless Vegetarian provides a Scratch Pad feature that allows visitors to write and save recipe ideas, ingredients, grocery lists, cooking notes, reminders, and other general notes.

To use the Scratch Pad, you may enter a username. The username is used to locate or create a Scratch Pad associated with that username.

The Scratch Pad may store the username and the content that you voluntarily enter into it. This information is used to provide the Scratch Pad functionality, including saving, retrieving, updating, and clearing your notes.

The Scratch Pad does not currently require an email address, password, real name, telephone number, or mailing address.

Because the current Scratch Pad uses a username rather than password-based authentication, it should not be considered a private or secure storage service. A username may be known or guessed by another person.

Please do not enter passwords, Social Security numbers, financial information, credit card information, medical information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive or confidential information into the Scratch Pad.

Usage Data

Usage Data may be collected automatically when you access or use the Service.

Depending on the technologies and services used by the Website, Usage Data may include information such as your Internet Protocol address, browser type, browser version, device information, pages visited, the date and time of your visit, time spent on pages, and other technical or diagnostic information.

Usage Data may be used to operate, maintain, secure, analyze, and improve the Website and its functionality.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Website may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, analyze website usage, and improve the user experience.

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies or notify you when cookies are being used.

Disabling certain cookies or browser storage may affect some Website functionality.

Third-party services used on the Website may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Website: To operate the Website and provide its features and content.

  • To provide the Scratch Pad: To create, retrieve, save, update, and clear Scratch Pad content associated with the username entered by a visitor.

  • To improve the Website: To understand website usage and improve our content, features, performance, and user experience.

  • To maintain security: To help detect, prevent, and address technical problems, misuse, fraud, or security issues.

  • To comply with legal obligations: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or valid governmental requests.

Third-Party Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to help operate, maintain, analyze, and improve the Website.

For example, the Scratch Pad uses Supabase as a third-party database service to store and retrieve Scratch Pad information.

Third-party service providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party services that you access through or interact with while using the Website.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent the information voluntarily entered into the Scratch Pad to other users for their own marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to operate the Website and provide its features.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, security, or safety of Fearless Vegetarian, our users, or others.

Information Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant Website functionality, maintain the Website, address security issues, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Scratch Pad content may remain stored until it is cleared by the user or otherwise deleted through the Website or through an applicable deletion request.

Deleting Your Information

If you use the Scratch Pad, you can clear the contents of your Scratch Pad using the Clear option provided on the Scratch Pad page.

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your Scratch Pad username, you may contact us through our Contact page.

We may retain certain information when required by law or when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Security of Your Information

We take reasonable measures to help protect information processed through the Website. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Because the Scratch Pad currently uses username-based access without password authentication, users should not store sensitive, confidential, or highly personal information in the Scratch Pad.

Sensitive Information

Fearless Vegetarian does not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the Website or the Scratch Pad.

Please do not enter passwords, Social Security numbers, financial account information, credit card information, medical information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive or confidential information into the Scratch Pad.

Children's Privacy

Our Website is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13.

We ask all visitors not to enter sensitive personal information into the Scratch Pad.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information through our Website, please contact us so that we can review the information and take appropriate action.

Links to Other Websites

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites, services, social media platforms, videos, or other external resources.

If you click a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's website. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every third-party website or service you visit.

Fearless Vegetarian does not control and is not responsible for the content, privacy policies, or practices of third-party websites or services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Website, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update this page and revise the Privacy Policy accordingly.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to request deletion of information associated with your Scratch Pad username, you can contact us through our Contact page.
